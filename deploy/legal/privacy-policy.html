Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

BaseMapped LLC (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the BaseCalc Plumbing mobile application (“BaseCalc Plumbing” or the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

BaseCalc Plumbing stores your job contacts, field tickets, calculations, and company settings locally on your device. No BaseCalc account or cloud sync is available, and the App does not send this user-entered business data to a BaseCalc cloud database.

  • Job contact records — names, companies, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses, and notes you choose to enter.
  • Field tickets and company details — work items, quantities, job state, notes, and the business information that appears on your field tickets.
  • Calculation history — inputs and results from plumbing calculations.

2. Advertising & Identifiers

This release does not display advertising, include an advertising SDK, or use an advertising identifier.

3. Purchases

BaseCalc Plumbing offers optional paid subscriptions for Pro access. Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and subscription entitlement status is managed through RevenueCat so the App can unlock Pro features. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

4. PDF Export & Sharing

When you generate a field-ticket PDF, it is created on your device. It is only shared if you explicitly choose to share it via email, messaging, SpeakSheet, or another app. You control who receives it.

5. Data Sharing & Sale

We do not sell your personal information. BaseCalc Plumbing does not share your job contacts, field tickets, company settings, or calculation history with a BaseCalc cloud database.

6. Your Choices & Data Deletion

You can delete individual job contacts, field tickets, and calculations in the App. You can also choose Settings > Local data > Delete all local data to remove App records, company details, generated worksheets still managed by the App, legacy App storage, and the legacy local database from that device.

Files you previously shared or copied to another app or location are not controlled by BaseCalc Plumbing and must be deleted there separately. Android automatic App backup is disabled. Copies already captured by an operating-system, device, or computer backup may remain in that backup until you delete it or it expires under the backup provider's rules. For the most complete device-level deletion, use the in-App deletion control before creating a backup, transferring the device, or uninstalling the App.

7. Children's Privacy

BaseCalc Plumbing is a professional tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
