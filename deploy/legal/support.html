BaseCalc Plumbing Support

Plumbing calculators, materials planning, and field-ticket records built for the job site.

Getting started

Open the Calculators tab to use 18 plumbing field tools for pipe hydraulics, drainage and vent checks, water-heater recovery, bounded gas sizing, pump head, fixture units, irrigation flow, thermal expansion, and related input checks. Supported results include the selected table or dimensional basis. When an input exceeds a modeled table, or a utility or manufacturer table is required, the App reports that limitation instead of guessing a size. Successful calculations are saved to History.

Jobs & field tickets

Use the Jobs tab to save job contacts and field tickets with workup items, site notes, and a field-ticket PDF. Use Materials for common pull-list reminders tied to the plumbing workflow.

SpeakSheet handoff

BaseCalc Plumbing is not the final billing system. When a job is ready to bill, share the field-ticket summary into SpeakSheet and create the customer invoice there.

Is my data private?

BaseCalc Plumbing works offline by default. Job contacts, field tickets, settings, and calculation history are stored locally on your device, and there is no BaseCalc cloud sync option. Use Settings > Local data > Delete all local data before a device transfer or backup when you need to remove App-managed records and generated files. See our Privacy Policy for backup and shared-file limitations.

A note on accuracy

BaseCalc Plumbing is a professional reference tool. Always verify results against current industry standards, manufacturer data, and local codes. The authority having jurisdiction has the final say.
